# Depel's Food Moments — Curated Image Direction

## Art direction
Aim for a contemporary, premium Nigerian food brand: editorial photography, modern kitchens, warm natural light, clean surfaces, sophisticated styling, and restrained green/gold accents. Avoid stereotypical or overly rustic scenes.

**People and representation:** Where people appear, prioritise Black/African people with a range of dark and brown skin tones in modern, believable homes and professional environments. A balanced set may include one image featuring a white person or family, but the people-focused images should not all be white. Ingredient-only and kitchen-appliance images are welcome and help create visual variety.

## Shortlisted starting points

1. **Family breakfast** — Black/African family sharing breakfast in a contemporary home. Check this Unsplash result for a suitable composition: https://unsplash.com/photos/man-woman-and-two-boys-inside-kitchen-P6ID8cthfVA
2. **Beans and kitchen** — Modern cooking/ingredient image; keep the clean gas-stove or oven image style the user liked. Browse: https://unsplash.com/s/photos/beans-cooking and https://unsplash.com/s/photos/kitchen-oven
3. **Baby Pap / caring moments** — Black/African parent and child in a bright, modern kitchen; natural, caring, unposed. Browse: https://unsplash.com/photos/young-african-woman-and-her-son-cooking-breakfast-or-lunch-by-kitchen-table-SgB-8CRziGE
4. **Honey breakfast** — Elegant close-up of honey and food, no people required. Browse: https://unsplash.com/s/photos/honey
5. **Soya and grains** — Premium ingredient-only photograph with grains/soya on a clean surface. Browse: https://unsplash.com/s/photos/soya-bean
6. **Business supply** — Black/African shop owner or food business professional in a modern, organised retail environment. Browse: https://www.pexels.com/search/african%20grocery%20store/

## Production checklist
- Select the actual individual photo, not just a search page.
- Check the specific image's license and any model/property release or trademark considerations.
- Do not imply a stock photo shows Depel's real packaging.
- Crop to the wide slider ratio (target 1280 × 260 px), keeping faces and key food details within the central safe area.
- Optimise each selected image to WebP, aiming below 500 KB.
- Store approved final assets in `public/images/moments/` using:
  `family-breakfast.webp`, `beans-kitchen.webp`, `baby-pap.webp`, `honey-breakfast.webp`, `soya-grains.webp`, `business-supply.webp`.
