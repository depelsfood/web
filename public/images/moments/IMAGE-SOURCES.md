# Depel's Food Moments — Image Resource Plan

These are temporary remote image sources for the six lifestyle slides. They are intended as visual placeholders while the site is being built; they do not depict Depel's actual packaging.

## Slide image sources

1. **Family breakfast** — Unsplash breakfast/food photography search: https://unsplash.com/s/photos/family-breakfast
2. **Beans and kitchen** — Unsplash food preparation and beans search: https://unsplash.com/s/photos/beans-cooking
3. **Baby pap / caring moments** — Unsplash parent-and-child lifestyle photography search: https://unsplash.com/s/photos/mother-baby-breakfast
4. **Honey breakfast** — Unsplash honey photography search: https://unsplash.com/s/photos/honey
5. **Soya and grains** — Unsplash soybeans photography search: https://unsplash.com/s/photos/soya-bean
6. **Business supply** — Unsplash grocery/food retail photography search: https://unsplash.com/s/photos/grocery-store-owner

## Brand and licensing checks

- Unsplash says its images are generally available for commercial and personal projects under the Unsplash License: https://help.unsplash.com/en/articles/2612315-can-i-use-unsplash-images-for-personal-or-commercial-projects
- Check each selected image for identifiable people, visible third-party logos, and any release requirements before final commercial use: https://help.unsplash.com/en/articles/2612329-releases-and-trademarks
- Stock lifestyle photos must not be presented as actual Depel's products or packaging.
- Final production preference: download approved selections, optimise to WebP (target under 500 KB each), and store in `public/images/moments/` so the site is not dependent on external image hosts.
